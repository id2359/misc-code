# OLYMPICA - compressed rules summary

> Condensed play aid based on the 1978 rules booklet. In a dispute, use the full rules.

## 1. Game and scenarios

1.1 **Aim:** U.N. forces must locate and capture the Web Generator; advanced play also permits evacuating it by Lifter.

1.2 **Introductory:** 4 turns. Web: 1 Generator, 2 Strongpoints, 10 Infantry, 3 dummies. U.N.: 3 Laser Tanks, 9 Heavy Infantry, 3 Light Infantry. No tunnels, BOAR or Lifters.

1.3 **Intro victory:** U.N. wins if a U.N. unit is stacked with the Generator at the end of turn 4; Web wins if this never occurs; otherwise draw.

1.4 **Advanced:** 8 turns. Web: 1 Generator, 4 Strongpoints, 17 Infantry, 4 dummies, 8 tunnels. U.N.: 6 Tanks, 19 Heavy Infantry, 4 Light Infantry, 3 Lifters, 1 BOAR.

1.5 **Advanced victory:** U.N. major = Generator evacuated by final turn; U.N. minor = U.N. unit stacked with it at game end; Web major = never stacked; Web minor = prevents both U.N. victories.

1.6 **Short Advanced:** 6 turns; Advanced forces/rules except no dummies and all Web units begin face-up; same victory conditions.

## 2. Map, counters and setup

2.1 Terrain is **clear**, **incline** or **cliff**. The marked five-hex-wide area is the Generator's **zone of uncertainty**.

2.2 A combat counter shows **range**, **combat strength (CS)** and **movement factor (MF)**. Parenthesised CS cannot make Ranged Combat attacks.

2.3 Setup order: choose forces/substitutions; Web setup; U.N. setup drops; begin turn 1.

2.4 Place the Generator in the zone of uncertainty. A Strongpoint may not share its hex. Observe stacking limits.

2.5 Web combat units/dummies begin face-down; tunnels do not. Reveal a unit when it moves, fires or is attacked; it remains face-up.

2.6 Setup drops may include any or none of the U.N. force. They move normally on turn 1. The Web may compel one eligible setup drop; then resolve avalanches and Close Assaults.

2.7 **Optional Advanced substitutions:** Web may exchange 1 Strongpoint + 1 dummy for 2 Infantry + 2 tunnels, or the reverse equivalent; U.N. always fields 23 infantry in any Heavy/Light mix allowed by the counters.

## 3. Turn sequence

3.1 **Web turn:** (a) compel one U.N. unit; (b) place tunnels; (c) return up to 4 Web Infantry; (d) move/Close Assault; (e) Ranged Combat; (f) remove DUST from all Web units.

3.2 **U.N. turn:** (a) drops; (b) move/Close Assault; (c) Ranged Combat; (d) remove DUST from all U.N. units; (e) Light Infantry moves/Close Assaults again.

3.3 Advance the turn marker after both player-turns.

## 4. Movement, terrain and stacking

4.1 Move units one at a time, up to MF hexes; unused MF is lost. A unit leaving the map cannot return.

4.2 A unit may pass through friendly units but may not normally finish stacked with one. It may stack with the Generator, a dummy or a tunnel marker.

4.3 Entering an enemy-occupied hex immediately ends movement and causes Close Assault. Units exert no zones of control.

4.4 **Incline:** Web Infantry unaffected. U.N. Infantry/Tanks stop on entry. BOAR is destroyed on entry. Lifters may fly over but not stop there.

4.5 **Cliff:** apply incline rules; additionally, U.N. units entering roll 1d6 and are eliminated on 6. BOAR/Lifter are automatically destroyed. Avalanche precedes Close Assault, except a Lifter assaults first.

4.6 No more than one unit may occupy a hex/level after movement, retreat or compulsion. Tunnel surface and underground portions are separate levels, each holding one unit.

## 5. Ranged Combat

5.1 During its Ranged Combat Phase, an eligible unit may attack once; a defender may be attacked repeatedly. Several attackers may combine against one defender; an attack never targets several defenders except as allowed in a Tunnel Mouth stack.

5.2 Count target hex as range 1. Fire ignores intervening units and terrain. Use full CS at any legal range.

5.3 Add attacker CS, compare with defender CS, reduce to an odds column and **round down in the defender's favour**; roll 1d6 on the CRT.

5.4 Inclines do not alter CS. In a cliff hex, CS is halved, fractions up, for attack and defence. Underground Tunnel-Mouth infantry is doubled against surface units; Tanks are not. Deep-Tunnel units cannot use or receive Ranged Combat.

## 6. Combat Results Table

| d6 | <=1:3 | 1:2 | 1:1 | 2:1 | 3:1 | >=4:1 |
|---:|:-----:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:-----:|
| 1 | AE | AE | EX | AR2 | DUST | DUST |
| 2 | AE | AE | AR2 | DUST | DUST | DR2 |
| 3 | AE | EX | DUST | DUST | DR2 | EX |
| 4 | AE | AR2 | DUST | DR2 | EX | DE |
| 5 | AR2 | DUST | DR2 | EX | DE | DE |
| 6 | DUST | DR2 | EX | DE | DE | DE |

6.1 **AE:** eliminate all attackers. In Ranged Combat, attackers ignore adverse results when attacking a parenthesised-CS unit; when a Tank/Strongpoint attacks infantry; and when a Strongpoint attacks a Tank from 7 hexes.

6.2 **DE:** eliminate the defender.

6.3 **AR2/DR2:** opposing player retreats affected unit(s) to a legal hex exactly two hexes from the original. No off-map retreat, friendly stacking or enemy entry unless no alternative; a Close Assault during retreat may satisfy the retreat after one hex.

6.4 Web units ignore terrain while retreating. U.N. units may retreat through cliff/incline but suffer avalanches; BOAR/Lifter are destroyed there. No unit retreats directly cliff-to-cliff. Strongpoints ignore retreats; underground units may ignore them. If DUST is followed by retreat, remove DUST and retreat.

6.5 **DUST:** mark defender; it cannot move in its next Movement Phase and its attack/defence CS is halved, fractions up. A cliff unit is halved only once. Extra DUST has no effect. Strongpoints, underground Tunnel-Mouth units and all Close Assaults ignore DUST.

6.6 **EX:** eliminate defender plus attacking units chosen by the attacker whose printed CS totals at least the defender's printed CS; if insufficient attackers participated, eliminate all attackers and defender. Ranged-combat immunity in 6.1 still applies; in Close Assault both units die.

## 7. Close Assault

7.1 Entering an enemy hex causes Close Assault. Remove a dummy immediately. If the Generator is the only Web counter there, capture it.

7.2 Otherwise defender rolls on the **4:1** CRT column against the assaulter, regardless of CS. On a roll of 1, ignore DUST and let the assaulter roll at 4:1 against the defender. Alternate until at least one unit is removed.

7.3 Close Assault ends the assaulter's movement, involves at most two combat units, ignores terrain and permits parenthesised-CS units. A unit may be Close Assaulted only once per Movement Phase.

7.4 Tunnel units may ignore retreat results. BOAR and Lifters may attack by Close Assault.

## 8. U.N. drops and Light Infantry

8.1 A drop contains exactly 3 units, each adjacent to the other two. It may land anywhere/on any terrain, on Web units, or above a Tunnel Mouth, but not underground.

8.2 A drop targeted wholly or partly within 2 hexes of the Generator may be compelled: translate its intact formation up to 3 hexes, keeping relative positions. Do not force it onto Web units if another legal placement exists; final placement may be no farther than 2 hexes from the Generator. One drop may be compelled during setup and one in each later Drop Phase.

8.3 A dropped BOAR/Lifter landing on cliff is destroyed automatically; another U.N. unit dies on a cliff only on a roll of 6. Landing on a Web unit reveals it and causes Close Assault.

8.4 Units dropped after setup cannot move or use Lifter transport that turn, but may fire. Light Infantry may use its second Movement Phase. Setup drops move normally on turn 1.

8.5 Light Infantry moves at full MF in both U.N. Movement Phases and may Close Assault in either. It may enter one cliff/incline in each phase.

## 9. Strongpoints and Laser Tanks

9.1 Strongpoints ignore retreat and DUST. When firing at range 7, they also ignore AE and EX.

9.2 Tanks and Strongpoints ignore AE, AR2 and EX when using Ranged Combat against infantry. Tanks underground in Tunnel Mouths ignore retreat/DUST but are not doubled.

9.3 **Optional defensive umbrella:** a Tank/Strongpoint protects friendly units in adjacent hexes from Ranged Combat unless every protecting Tank/Strongpoint is also attacked that phase. No protection against Close Assault. A face-down Strongpoint provides no umbrella at the start of the Ranged Phase; Web may voluntarily reveal it during movement.

## 10. Tunnels - Advanced

10.1 Web has 8 tunnel markers. Place Tunnel Mouths initially in 1717, 1818, 1010 and 1009; place the remaining 4 Mouths/Deep Tunnels in Web Tunnel Phases.

10.2 Mouths must be in cliff/incline. A Deep Tunnel must adjoin a Mouth or connected Deep Tunnel. No tunnel may share a Strongpoint hex; tunnels never move.

10.3 When placing a tunnel, Web units there may be put above or below it; remove their DUST if put underground. U.N. units cannot be placed underground this way.

10.4 Legal 1-MF tunnel movement includes adjacent connected underground hexes, underground Mouth to/from its surface or an adjacent surface hex, and normal movement between the Mouth's surface and adjacent surface hexes.

10.5 Ignore surface terrain and surface enemy units when moving underground, but a surface enemy blocks entry to/exit from that Mouth. U.N. Infantry/Tanks may go underground; BOAR/Lifters may not. Surface units may pass over underground enemies.

10.6 In a Tunnel Mouth, underground infantry doubles CS in Ranged Combat against surface enemies; Tanks do not. Underground units ignore AR2, DR2 and DUST. Deep-Tunnel units cannot make or receive Ranged Combat.

10.7 If units occupy both Mouth levels, the lower unit cannot be separately attacked from another hex; opposing levels may fire at each other, while outside fire targets only the surface unit. The two levels cannot be attacked together.

10.8 Close Assault works normally within tunnels, but tunnel units may ignore retreats. An enemy below a Mouth cannot assault its surface if another enemy is already there, and vice versa.

10.9 **Optional demolition:** spend the entire Movement Phase underground, then in Ranged Combat roll 4-6 to remove that tunnel and move the unit to an adjacent legal hex. Do not sever the only surface connection, demolish the Generator's hex, or attack that turn; trapped enemies survive.

## 11. BOAR - Advanced

11.1 BOAR is destroyed by entering cliff/incline from any cause. Once grounded, Web movement compulsion cannot move it.

11.2 Adjacent to BOAR: Web units under Tunnel Mouths lose their doubling; Strongpoints halve CS for attack/defence (a cliff Strongpoint is halved, not quartered).

11.3 To drill, end movement above a Deep Tunnel and immediately roll 4-6; replace it with a Tunnel Mouth. BOAR cannot drill on its drop turn. Failure may be retried next U.N. Movement Phase.

11.4 After successful drilling, unmoved U.N. units may use the new Mouth immediately. Units underground in a drilled Mouth cannot make or receive Ranged Combat.

## 12. Lifters - Advanced

12.1 Lifter MF is 25. It may pick up/drop U.N. units anywhere along its route at no movement cost; no unit may remain aboard after movement.

12.2 A transported unit may move before **or** after transport, not both; only one Lifter may carry it per turn. Transport is allowed after Close Assault or while DUSTed, but not on its drop turn. Lifters cannot carry Web units.

12.3 Capacity through any one hex is combined CS 50. A Lifter entering a Web-occupied hex Close Assaults. Normal stacking is checked only at movement end.

12.4 Lifters may fly over cliff/incline but cannot pick up, drop or finish there; stopping, dropping, retreating or being compelled there destroys them. They cannot enter tunnels or handle units underground in Tunnel Mouths.

12.5 **Evacuation:** if a U.N. unit starts a U.N. turn stacked with the Generator and a Lifter begins in an adjacent hex, the Lifter may collect it and leave the map by a clear route. If underground, the Lifter must use an adjacent Mouth with an unblocked path to the Generator.

## 13. Web Generator, dummies and reinforcements

13.1 **Movement compulsion:** in each Web Compulsion Phase move one U.N. unit except BOAR up to 3 hexes, regardless of MF. It need not start near the Generator and may be in a tunnel. It cannot leave the map, stack with a friendly unit or enter an enemy hex unless no legal alternative. It stops after Close Assault.

13.2 Compelled movement may enter any terrain: Lifters are destroyed in cliff/incline; other U.N. units roll for avalanche but need not stop there. No cliff-to-cliff compulsion. Web units cannot be compelled.

13.3 Once a U.N. unit ends its turn stacked with the Generator, both forms of compulsion are permanently disabled, even if that unit later leaves or is destroyed.

13.4 Generator starts in the uncertainty zone, cannot move except by Lifter evacuation, may be underground, does not count for stacking and cannot attack or be attacked. Units stacked with it may be attacked normally.

13.5 Dummies cannot move or attack, do not count for stacking and are removed when attacked by either combat type.

13.6 In each Web Reinforcement Phase return up to 4 eliminated Web Infantry: east edge on odd turns, west edge on even turns. The edge hex counts as the first MF; entrants may use full MF. Excess eligible units wait. No force may exceed its initial Infantry total; no other eliminated counter returns.
